Privacy Policy

This page explains the privacy basics for visitors to the VETER website.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Information and services

VETER includes informational pages, browser games, developer tools and community features. Different features may process information differently. Do not enter sensitive personal information into public profiles, posts, prompts or forms.

Information handled by the site

When you visit a website, hosting, security and performance services may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and timestamps. If you use a feature that asks for information, that information may be processed to provide the feature.

Cookies and advertising

Some embedded services, analytics or advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies. Their use is governed by their own policies and settings. Browser settings can help you control cookies.

External links

VETER may link to third-party websites. VETER does not control their privacy practices; review their policies before using them.

Children

The website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has submitted personal information can contact the project.

Your choices and contact

You can choose not to use optional features or share information. For a privacy question or request, email harshith934764@gmail.com. We may need enough information to understand and verify a request.

Policy updates

This policy may change as the website and its services evolve. The latest version will be posted on this page.

This is a general website privacy notice, not a legal compliance certification. The project owner should review it against the actual hosting, analytics, advertising, authentication and data-retention setup.
