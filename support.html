How to report a useful bug

  1. Tell us which VETER page or product you used.
  2. Include your device, operating system and browser.
  3. Write the steps that caused the problem.
  4. Tell us what you expected and what happened instead.
  5. Add a screenshot only after hiding personal or sensitive information.

Never send passwords, one-time codes, payment card details, API keys or private account information through public issues or social media.
