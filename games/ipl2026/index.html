Sim The Whole Season?

Every remaining league fixture — including yours — will be simulated instantly by the AI. Results apply to the table, caps and records exactly as if played. This cannot be undone.

Squads: Complete, verified IPL 2026 25-player rosters (post-auction, December 2025 — CSK · MI · KKR · RCB · DC · RR · PBKS · SRH · LSG · GT).  Formats: FULL XI CLASSIC (11 players, max 4 overseas, innings ends at 10 wickets / 6 balls) or ONE-OVER SHOWDOWN (4 batters + 3 bowlers per side, innings ends at 2 wickets).  Flow: Format → Team 1 → Team 2 → Team 1 XI → Team 2 XI → Confirm → Toss → Bat/Bowl → AI vs AI one-over match (2D animated broadcast view).  Your control: You pick every player yourself — or tap AI Quick Pick and edit the result freely; the AI never swaps anyone without your tap. Player photos are stylized avatars (official headshots are not redistributable). Ratings are editorial game ratings out of 99.
